Sources and data notes

Weather: air temperature, weather condition, precipitation chance, visibility, wind speed, direction and gusts, from the Open-Meteo Forecast API, which combines national weather services including the UK Met Office and DWD Germany.

Waves and sea temperature: wave height and period, and sea surface temperature, from the Open-Meteo Marine API.

Tide: high and low tide times shown here are estimated from Open-Meteo's modelled sea level height, not from an official tide table. This is not a substitute for an authoritative source. For confirmed UK tide times, check ADMIRALTY EasyTide directly.

Open-Meteo is free for non-commercial use and requires no API key. Both data sets are fetched live from your browser each time you refresh this page.

This is a training planning aid, not a safety certification. Always check conditions on the day and follow local safety guidance.
